This is a sample post, mostly so I have something to look at while I learn
how posts work here before I write anything real.

A few things worth remembering about this setup:

- The filename has to be `YYYY-MM-DD-title-with-dashes.markdown` — the date
  in the filename is what Jekyll uses to sort posts and build the URL
  (`/blog/2026/08/13/why-im-building-this-the-boring-way/`).
- `tags` in the front matter above is optional metadata. There's no
  tag-browsing page yet — I'm deliberately not building one until I
  actually have enough posts for it to matter — but the data will already
  be there when I do.
- Everything below the second `---` is plain Markdown. This bullet list,
  for instance, is just three lines starting with `-`.

Delete this post once there's something real to publish in its place.
